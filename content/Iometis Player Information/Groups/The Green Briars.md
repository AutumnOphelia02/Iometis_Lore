#Organization 

The Green Briars are the only protection guild of [[Midovici]]. Though they were ousted some 50 years ago by [[Leszek Ecskmanna]] and the [[Impures]]. Since then they have fractured and splinted off into many different groups, most went on to find work else where forgetting their old group. Many set out as freelance adventurers and mercenaries, and a few even turned to banditry as a way to make ends meet.
