Aviseau is the largest continuous continent in this hemisphere of Iometis. Dominated by a great mountain range in its central region with many plateaus and valleys and vast hilly plains surrounding it. Off the South-Eastern coast of central Aviseau lies the [[Kinoan Island Overview|Kinoan Islands]] and off the Eastern Coast of Southern Aviseau lies [[Jraic Overview|Jraic]].

The nation of [[Zamonia Overview|Zamonia]] Dominates much of the hilly plains East of the great mountains.
The nation of [[Cetel Overview|Cetel]] occupies the small island of the coast of Aviseau, nearly due North of the [[Kinoan Island Overview|Kinoan Islands]].
