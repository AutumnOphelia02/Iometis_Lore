The Astral Whole is the national religion of [[Zamonia Overview|Zamonia]]. The worship reveres the stars and celestial sphere. It denies the divinity of the Maeserai, labeling them as [[Heathen Gods]] and instead believes that divinity comes from the celestial sphere, that when you die if you have lived a pure life you will be placed among the heavens as a star in the great tapestry of the Astral Whole. The Astral Whole doctrine states that like the celestial sphere we all have our constellation we are a part of and a role to play in society. The Astral Whole tracks births and the constellations children are born under. It is believed that these stars indicate many things about the children and use it as a basis for many aspects of Zamonian society. The Astral Whole tracks not only the celestial sphere of Iometis, but also [[The Orrery of Iometis|The Orrery]] of Planes, with children born under different planes being especially auspicious, or damned.

Astral diviners and astronomers are highly valued and often hold great political sway, interpreting the stars and planes guiding the nation to divine glory.
