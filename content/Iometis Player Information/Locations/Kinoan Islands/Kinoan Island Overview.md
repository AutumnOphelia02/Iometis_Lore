The Kinoan Islands is a large group of islands off the southern coast of North-Eastern Aviseau. It is comprised of three main islands: [[Kinos Overview|Kinos]] (the largest), [[Akenenos Overview|Akenenos]], and [[Pelkea Overview|Pelkea]] (the smallest). With many more islands scattered around these three.  

The Kinos Islands are characterized by wide tracks of wilderness dotted with small settlements. Larger towns and cities are not uncommon but are often very far apart. The dense and prolific forests of these islands makes it difficult to travel without a road making their upkeep and safety of the utmost importance. Often larger cities provide such services in exchange for goods produced in the smaller villages such as food, stone, lumber, animal products, and textiles.  

The three islands effectively function as three separate nations though often people from beyond the islands treat them as a single nation. While technically the three islands are each their own nation there is little legal or bureaucratic power holding them together. There is not strict taxing or a single leader that runs the nation but rather each community runs itself as it sees best. Larger cities often tax nearby smaller settlements for resources in exchange for upkeep and patrolling of the roads connecting the two settlements. Refusal to pay such tax doesn’t result in legal action but simply an end of work maintaining and patrolling said roadways.  

Most communities are run by a small council of locals. Depending on the settlement these councils consist of elected members or simply the wealthiest or most powerful people in the area. Most cities have guilds or groups dedicated to protection of the city and these groups are usually hired to patrol the roads. Groups that have lost power or favor in cities can often find jobs elsewhere with their martial or magical prowess but some do turn to banditry to eke out a living.  

People who study and learn magical arts often find themselves in positions of power, be it for their ability to magically solve mundane problems, their ability to defend, protect, and attack outside threats, or simply their ability to alter the hearts and minds of those around them. Of course people with this power can abuse it and as a result some communities strictly forbade certain or all magicks
