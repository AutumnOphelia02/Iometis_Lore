#Location 

Bander
--
Population: ~570
Demographics: 32% Gnomish, 30% Halfling, 23% Ysoki, 10% Tripkee, 5% Other

Overview
--
Bander is a small fishing and farming community. Located the Mysaea Dale along the Thirjin River. What started as a small community of fishers and farmers has now evolved to meet the needs of the nearby magical city of [[Sobraca]]. Providing not only fish and produce to the city but also farmable components for spells. Bats are kept and their guano collected and sold to the wizards on the high mountain peaks. In return Bander benefits from magical artifice from the College of Civics and from regular patrols from the College of Spellcraft keeping the riverways to [[Sobraca]] and the surrounding wilderness safe from monsters and banditry.

Government
--
Bander has little overarching government. A small and communal village most disputes are resolved by the authority of those involved. Larger disputes do happen but are rare and usually are settle by direct democratic decisions of the community. Bander is defined by its communal philosophies. Food, shelter, and medicine is something that all of the villagers work together to provide for everyone. There is little family structures too, birthing members of the community may or may not be heavily involved in the raising of the young and it falls on the entire village to care and teach for the younger members.
