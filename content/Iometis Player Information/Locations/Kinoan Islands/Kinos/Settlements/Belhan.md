Belhan
--
Population: ~500
Demographics: 37% Human, 21% Halfling, 14% Dwarf, 11% Elf, 10% Goblin, 4% Orc, 3% Other

Overview
--
Belhan is a small farming village that provides crops for many of the surrounding villages. Primarily it deals with [[Roke]] for quarried stone and ore, [[Ish’conach]] for hides and meat, and [[Oroszko]] for lumber. Like many settlements in the region it sends a tax of its crops to [[Midovici]] to ensure the larger city maintains and patrols the roads around it.

Government
--
Belhan has little overarching government, most people keep to themselves and help the community when they can. Crime isn’t terribly common and threats from beyond the village are dealt with by the Impures. But when things do take a turn for the worst most people turn to the Maven, a title held by the local Druid who helps make sure the earth is tended to, the crops grow healthy, and the settlement doesn’t take more than needed. The Maven is consulted when the village is expanded, when weather or circumstance ruins crops, or when crimes have been committed. Generally the Maven acts alone, under their own guidance, but when crimes have been committed a small group is usually rounded up to determine what recompense is necessary. Generally the victim is compensated for lost items if they cannot be returned. For serious crimes perpetrators can be exiled or even executed, though it has been many decades since such a crime has been committed. The Maven is a title that is passed down from druid to druid. The current maven trains and teaches an apprentice who then takes over when the old maven dies or retires.
The Current Maven is [[Runlain Ironroot]] a Female (She/her) Dwarf

History
--
Belhan is an old settlement, what began as a collection of hunters slowly grew, as more people joined and built houses more of the surrounding forest was cut down and cleared. The rich soil between the two lakes provided fertile land for farming and soon fields were plowed and crops planted. Slowly the town expanded to its current size, clearing more forest for farmland and using the wood for buildings and as trade for other materials. The town has since introduce many different crops and animals. Now farms boast crops from wheat, beans, and corn, to potatoes, carrots, and onions. Many farmers also keep cattle, sheep, pigs, and many other livestock. The village has taken on the role of feeding much of the surrounding villages, making it a common stop for traders between the villages. The position of Maven is as old as the village itself, indeed the founder of the village was the first Maven, a druid of old who lead the group of hunters from Boromos to build their own home. This first Maven built the town around the Grey Stone which still rests in the center of town and is marked with the names of the first people who settled here.

Rumors, Legends, and Stories
--
The Stricken: Legend says there resides a creature in the lake south of Belhan, a terrible beast of impossible beauty that lures all who wander close into the water where it drowns and devours them, but even death cannot stop the siren call and the bodies of those who have fallen before pull down new victims

The Maven has been searching hard for an apprentice recently, this has sparked rumors about her health. Some say she portended her own death with old magic of divination, others say she caught a rare disease that she is slowly succumbing to, and yet others still say that she discovered an ancient curse that she needs help containing until it can be more permanently fixed.

Old Man Horn: Legend tells of a creature that walks the forest around Belhan, Old Man Horn, with the body of a deer that walks on two legs, its arms ending in clawed hands rather than hooves and rows of sharp fangs protruding from its mouth. Where his eyes would be is nothing but empty sockets which hold white smoke that glows dimly, but more than this Old Man Horn has two large antlers sprouting from the top of their head that constantly drip blood. Old Man Horn hates any kind of light and hides constantly in the shadows, where, from the light of his eyes, only his face can dimly be seen. He is said to devour any he can, and takes a fondness in eating villagers from Belhan, whom he has never forgiven for destroying so much of his forest. It is said an offering of a goat marked with the sign of Old Man Horn will placate the creature and keep you safe against his stalking.
