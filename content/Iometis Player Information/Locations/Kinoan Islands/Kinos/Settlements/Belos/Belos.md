# Overview
A holy city where [[Aratea]] herself once sat and tended to her Moon Grove. After the creation of the weave the city has been left in the care of Aratea's followers who attempt to run the city in much the same way as The Silver Moon themself. Aratea's tenets of freedom and individuality have attracted many artists, free thinkers, and philosophers; in addition to those faithful to the deity herself.
