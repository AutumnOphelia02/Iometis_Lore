#Location 


Ish’conach
Population: ~475
Demographics: 31% Gnome, 28% Elf, 21% Goblin, 16% Human, 4% Other

# Overview
Ish’conach is a small hunting and fishing village that provides meat and hides for many of the nearby villages. Trading their animal products for crops from [[Belhan]], quarried stone and ore from [[Roke]], and lumber from [[Oroszko]]. Like many settlements in the region it sends a tax of its goods to [[Midovici]] to ensure the larger city maintains and patrols the roads around it.

# Government
Ish’conach is run by the wealthy elite of the village, notably [[Peckran Surx]] who owns all of the docks, most of the fishing grounds, and a few hunting grounds. Those who want to keep their boat at a dock must pay for their spot, if they want to hunt or fish on his grounds they must purchase a license or surrender a tithe from their haul. While there are a few other people who own hunting grounds or a scattered few fishing grounds none have as many holdings as [[Peckran Surx|Surx]] and many of them are losing their own holdings to his growing wealth. [[Peckran Surx|Surx]] employs his own [[Surx's Guard|personal guard]] which acts as the village's police, which largely means enforcing [[Peckran Surx|Surx]]’s holdings, arresting those who hunt or fish on his grounds without the proper license or who dock their ship without paying for the privilege. They leave the defense of the village from external threats up to the [[Impures]] focusing on maintaining [[Peckran Surx|Surx]]’s order inside the village. While the [[Surx's Guard|guards]] of the village work directly for [[Peckran Surx|Surx]] they maintain a facade of being there to protect order and safety of the village, they enforce the land holdings of other wealthy individuals, and do investigate crimes and punish those they find guilty. Though most crimes perpetrated against the common folk slip through the cracks.
[[Peckran Surx|Surx]] pretends, and perhaps even believes himself, that he is making the village a better place. He believes he, through managing its wealth and resources, will make this a great city and bring in wealth to everyone. Though in reality the only person seeing the wealth is himself and those working directly for him.

# History
Ish’conach was founded as a simple fishing village long ago, like many villages in the region it can trace its founding back to Boromos. This village looked much like Belhan, with a druid providing guidance and leadership when needed. However, almost three centuries ago the Druid unexpectedly passed before training an apprentice. This loss of leadership and protection led to increased trade and stockpiles, without a druid to sustain the population during harsh winters and bad harvests the town needed supplies from traders and large store houses. As the town grew certain fortunate members of the community had better harvests, which led to larger store rooms, more goods to trade with and more power in the village. This system worked relatively well for many generations but about 40 years ago [[Peckran Surx]], a trader from Midovici decided he wanted to stay. Claiming he saw potential in this village and that he wanted to make it great he bought land and began building his fortune. His original mercenary guard that protected his trade caravan became the villages first guards and slowly he began changing things in the village, first he reasoned that if people could buy land to build a house they could buy land for other purposes too, and in doing so he began buying docks to rent out to people to store their boats. Next he began buying portions of the lake and regions of the forest as hunting grounds. Others in the town began doing the same, the fortunate few who could began buying regions of the lake and forest too, but Surx had too much money from his days as a trader to lose the bidding war and over time he came to control the majority of the village, and brought it into the state it currently is.

# Rumors, Legends, and Stories
The Curse of Aibhinn: The last druid to serve Ish’conach was Aibhinn, legend holds that she committed anathema against nature and was cursed by a Fey Noble. This is what took her life but the Fey was crueler than that, dooming Ish’conach to centuries of strife and hardship. The wealthy of the Village say it is nothing more than an old story, but those who suffer under the heel of the elite have to wonder, is this village cursed to suffer, doomed to be an enemy of the natural world? Legend holds that one day a Druid will return to Ish’conach, right the wrongs of Aibhinn, and restore balance to the village.

Rumor has it that Surx made a deal with a devil to earn the fortune that built his manor and business in Ish’conach. This is why he settled here, because in Midovici his deal was discovered so he had to flee elsewhere with his new found fortune. They say that when his home was built he performed a ritual to protect it and that the ritual involved mixing bones of the innocent into the walls of his home.

Court of Noble Fey: It is said that near the village of Ish’conach is the court of the noble fey that Cursed Aibhinn. Impossible to find without the will of the Fey themselves this court is known by the circle of white-spotted purple mushrooms, upon stepping inside the ring of fungus the court appears and only a lucky few escape the still boiling wrath of the Scorned Fey.
