#TownDistrict 

District Type: Military

# Overview

Dockwatch is the only military district amongst the Dockside districts. Its home to several watch posts and barracks all run by the [[Impures]]. It maintains Midovici Law in these districts, though it only truly patrols in the [[Midovici Docks]] making sure the goods coming and going from Midovici are safely handled. The Surrounding Districts are often left to themselves, for good or bad, with only occasional patrols passing through.
