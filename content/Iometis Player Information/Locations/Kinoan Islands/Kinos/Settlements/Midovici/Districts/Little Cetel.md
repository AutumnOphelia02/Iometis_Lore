# Overview
Little Cetel is a district made up almost entirely of Cetelish Immigants. Cetel is a small island nation off the Eastern side of Aviseau. As the nation has grown within the boundaries of the small island it occupies many of its people have set off for new locations, a fresh start, less crowded cities, or simply away from a nation whose ideals they don't agree with. Many of these people have found their way to the Kinos islands and Kinos in particular. Entering into Midovici a semi-large community of Cetelish people created their own community outside of Midovici proper, one that continues to grow as more immigrants enter the nation.
