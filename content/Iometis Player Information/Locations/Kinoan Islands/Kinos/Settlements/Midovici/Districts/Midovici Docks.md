District Type: Mercantile

# Overview

The Midovici Docks are a trade hub of the town. With goods coming in from all over Iometis, though mostly from Aviseau, Akenenos, and Pelkea. More than just goods come through this entry port however, with immigrants and travellers alike exiting ships hoping to find better fortunes here in Kinos. The docks are home to many trading companies as well as a disproportionate amount of taverns and inns. There are few shops in this mercantile district and most of them are fishmongers or shipping companies. With most of the goods off loaded in the docks taken to Midovici Proper to be sold in some of the higher end markets, there are some who trade within the Dockside districts to those who can afford it.
