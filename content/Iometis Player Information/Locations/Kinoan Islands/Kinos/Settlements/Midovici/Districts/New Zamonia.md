# Overview
New Zamonia is a district made up primarily of Zamonian refugees. Fleeing their home country due  to the new law created with the coronation of Emperor Irido Gol and the transition to the Religion of the Lightless Flame. This religion refutes the existance of other deities in favor for the one true god, the Lightless Flame. Emperor Gol required conversion of all of his people and imprisoned, executed, or tortured those who did not convert. This led to a mass exodus of many people of Zamonia, many of whom ended up in Kinos, specifically Midovici. New Zamonia is made up almost entirely of these refugees. They, like many others, were not wholly welcomed into Midovici proper and as such have created a community for themselves on the periphory of the town.
