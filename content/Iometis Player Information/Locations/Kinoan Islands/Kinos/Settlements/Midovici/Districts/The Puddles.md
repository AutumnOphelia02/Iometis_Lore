# Overview
The Puddles is located on the eastern side of the dockside districts northward of the coastal forest outside of [[Midovici]] proper. Similar to the scapes it is home to those who have been pushed to the periphory of [[Midovici]]. The overcrowded tumble of buildings that makes up the district are home to many desperate people. When not being ignore by the [[Impures]] the people of this district are often abused by them. Resulting in little if any trust between the populace and the government of the city. Instead the populace turns to various crime syndicates and mobsters for protection and assistance. Notably [[Naguk]], an orcish mobster who controls the majority of the district. Her tendency to look out for marginalized people have made her very popular amongst the people of the puddles, if still feared.  Other than [[Naguk]], the most powerful crime lord is [[Ssevsk]] a lizardfolk crime lord who controls much territory on the boundary between the puddles and [[Trade Quarter]]. This has given [[Ssevsk]] an advantage over [[Naguk]] in their attempts to gain a foothold in the [[Trade Quarter]].
