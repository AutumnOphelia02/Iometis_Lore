#TownDistrict 

District Type: Slums

# Overview
The southern most district amongst the dockside districts. Located near the coastal forest outside of [[Midovici]], the scrapes is an overcrowded district with many people who have been pushed out of other districts or who can't afford housing elsewhere. The district is largely ignored by the Impures and the uppercrust of [[Midovici]] in general, instead local crime bosses and mobsters run various operations and protect various people in the district. Cheif among them in this distrcit is [[Borax]], a goblin mobster whose community orientated mindset has resulted in widespread support of many of his schemes. Though there are those who challenge his authority, notably [[Velesh of Kreia]] a crime lord who controlls the next largest portion of the district
