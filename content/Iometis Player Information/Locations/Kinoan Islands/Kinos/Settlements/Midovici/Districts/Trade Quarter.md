# Overview
The Trade Quarter is the largest mercantile district amongst the dockside districts. It is home to many shops, trade guilds, and market squares for the various districts of Dockside. Being situated inbetween the Puddles and the Scrapes various mobsters and gang leaders vie for control of various parts of the district and several turf wars are waged every few years as different people get bolder. This oft neglected mercantile district is known for being a place where any good can be found, if not found legally. The dockwatch patrols rarely come through this district and as such protection from various crime leaders often falls to other crime leaders resulting in a web of factions between the various merchants and residents of the district and the crime lords who run things.
