# Overview
Wharfside is a poorer neighborhood in Midovici. Originally built around the homes of the dock workers and fishers of the town it has slowly grown as overcrowding in the city spills out to the dockside districts. It has also grown with the influx of immigrants from Aviseau. It remains a district largely made up of dock workers and fishers but the ethinic makeup has diversified much in recent years.
