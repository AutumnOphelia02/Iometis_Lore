#Location

Population: ~15,000
Demographics: 25% Human, 13% Gnome, 12% Elf, 10% Dwarf, 9% Amurrun (Catfolk), 9% Ysoki (Rat folk), 8% Orc, 6% Goblin, 5%, Halfling, 3% other

Overview
-----------------
One of the largest cities in the region, the port city of Midovici acts as the entry point to Kinos from elsewhere. Because of this it has become a melting pot of the region and a hub of trade for the island. Most goods that cannot be found on the island have passed through Midovici at some point.
Midovici's Protection Guild Patrols, maintains, and protects the roads between Midovici, [[Oroszko]], [[Ish’conach]], [[Belhan]], and [[Roke]].

Government
-----------------------
Midovici is currently run by the [[Diamond Circle]], a group of four casters: The Druid [[Bala Foreg]], the Cleric [[Tristan Kempwell]], the Bard [[Kaelyr Vrsk]], and the Wizard [[Leszek Ecskmanna]]. These four people in theory are supposed to use their power for the good of the city, but often individual pursuits of power or wealth take the forefront. Each member of the [[Diamond Circle]] has their own counsel, usually consisting of other practitioners of their magical tradition. It is usually from these councils that new members of the [[Diamond Circle]] are chosen when one dies or retires. To be considered for a position of the [[Diamond Circle]] one must be nominated by a current member, usually the head of their tradition, but it isn’t unheard of for an ambitious person to seek nomination from another tradition in order to replace their mentor. Heads of each tradition keep their seat until they choose to retire, they die, or they are voted out by all three of the other tradition heads, a rare occasion to be sure.

The current protection guild of Midovici is the [[Impures]]. They maintain security in the city as well as patrol the roads to all villages who pay Midovici a tax. The [[Impures]] got their name from the original group they spawned from. A small group of people, who made a deal with an unknown entity for power, after the deal was struck one of their members left, saying that the group tainted their purpose and had become Impure. The rest of the group took it as their new name. The Impures took over as Midovici’s protection guild ~50 years ago replacing the old guild ([[The Green Briars]]) with the help of Leszek Ecskmanna. Whom they continue to help. The Impures are renowned throughout the region for being very efficient at their job. Eagerly going out and hunting down threats to the various settlements under their protection, from Dire Animals to monstrous creatures to bandits.

Districts
--------------
The districts of Midovici are broken up into two main sections, Dockside and The Old Town. The Old Town consists of many different districts, most of which are inside the walled safety of Midovici Proper. Dockside encapsulates the districts around the docks of Midovici which have largely grown from the immigrants from Aviseau.

Dockside includes the districts: [[Midovici Docks]], [[Dockwatch]], [[Wharfside]], [[New Zamonia]], [[Little Cetel]], [[Trade Quarter]], [[The Puddles]], [[The Scrapes]]

The Old Town includes the districts: [[Beggar's Watch]], [[Northside]], [[Copperside]], [[Northruns]], [[Silverside]], [[Elmwood]], [[Tome's Corner]], [[Hillside]], [[Gatewatch]], [[Southside]], [[Fieldside]], [[The Coins]], [[Old Midovici]], [[Watchside]], [[Woodside]], [[Walltop]], [[Chrysallis District]], [[The Diamond Ring]], [[Traveller's Row]], [[Idelntown]], [[Foreign Quarter]], [[Northgate]], [[Three Corners]]  

History
-------------
Midovici was formed from the mixing of Native Kinoans and immigrants from Aviseau. What started as a large fishing village grew exponentially as trade between the two nations picked up. Now Midovici is one of the largest cities on Kinos, only truly rivaled by Boromos. As the common entry point to Kinos from Aviseau it is a hub of trade, ideas, and people.
The [[Diamond Circle]] originated from an old druidic order that had four members: Speaker of the Earth, Speaker of the Water, Speaker of the Wind, and Speaker of the Fire. As the city grew and innovations of arcane and occult magic and blessings from the divine helped the city grow and prosper and the powerful members of those groups wanted a say in the direction and regulations of the city. Over time the four druids were consolidated down to a single druid and other practitioners of different magical traditions replaced the other three druids.

Rumors, Legends, and Stories
-----------------------------------------------------
The Ghost of Light Keeper: Legend says the light house is haunted by the ghost of its first keeper who was killed by a grieving husband after his spouse was drowned in a ship wreck due to the lighthouse not being lit one evening. The Keeper now cannot rest while the light house is dark, and must spend eternity burning the fires to light the way for ships.

Rumor has it that the Diamond Circle is planning something big, and dangerous, speculation says they are preparing for war but against who is the harder question to answer.

People have been disappearing from the poorer districts of the city recently, no one knows where they go or what has happened to them, most assume they ran away or died an unknown death somewhere, but those who knew them insist that something nefarious is afoot.
