# Overview
Mura’iti is a small community of roughly 15 families located northeast of [[Ish’conach]]. It is often considered a part of the larger village. Mura’iti, like [[Ish’conach]], predominantly subsists from hunting and foraging, trading hides and meat for that which they don’t or can’t produce. While Mura’iti isn’t taxed by [[Midovici]] it is close enough to Ish’conach that it benefits from most of the protection. However it isn’t uncommon for individual families to make deals of their own with various groups for protection.

# Government
Mura’iti is so small that it doesn’t have an overarching government. People take care of themselves and help out neighbors when they can, if they can. If any issues crop up that require more mediation, the people of Mura’iti can travel the short distance to [[Ish’conach]] where they can bring their issues up with [[Peckran Surx|Surx]] and his judiciaries. Though the known corruption of [[Peckran Surx|Surx]] often dissuades people from making the trip except for the most extreme cases.

# History
Mura’iti is a relatively new settlement, roughly 35 years ago a group of hunters from Ish’conach decided the village was proceeding in a direction they did not want to follow. To escape the taxes for docks, the permits for hunting, and all the other expenses that were being created by Surx these people left, just far enough to be able to hunt and farm for free, but close enough to still benefit from the Midovici patrols. Since then the village has gained a family or two but largely has remained a simple hunting village that sustains itself and nothing more.

# Rumors, Legends, and Stories
Mother Bear: Legend says that the mother of all bears resides in a cave near Mura’iti. Hunter’s have tried to bring the beast down but all have failed and only the lucky few have escaped with their lives. Now the villagers avoid the area and hope that the bear doesn’t wander too close to their homes.

The Curse of Surx: Rumor has it that [[Peckran Surx|Surx]] was bitter from the villagers leaving and denying him his profit, so once again he turned to dark forces to curse the village. While the village doesn’t seem to have suffered any ill effects from this curse it is still fun to blame every minor inconvenience on a dark curse from a corrupt leader. 

Rumor has it that things have gotten worse in Ish’conach recently and that even those who want to leave are barred from doing so. What can one do against the trained mercenaries of Surx.
