#Location 

Oroszko
Population: ~750
Demographics: 34% Orc, 32% Goblin, 15% Elf, 9% Dwarf, 8% Human, 2% Other

Overview
Oroszko is a small foresting village that provides lumber and produce for many of the nearby villages. Trading wood and the fruits of their orchards for crops from [[Belhan]], quarried stone and ore from [[Roke]], and meat and animal hides from [[Ish’conach]]. Like many settlements in the region it sends a tax of its goods to [[Midovici]] to ensure the larger city maintains and patrols the roads around it.

Government
Oroszko is run by the Town Council. The Town Council is comprised of two parts, the Head and the Hands. The Hands of the Council are elected officials that take care of everyday issues that crop up. Dealing with simple land disputes, collection of the Midovici tax, building permits, and other such issues. Hands of the Council are elected by popular vote of every adult from the village and hold office for 5 years. The Head of the Council is a civic duty of every citizen. When larger, more important decisions must be made, such as criminal trials, creation of new laws, trade agreements with neighboring villages, and other such issues, a raffle is held in which 11 citizens are selected to participate as the Head of the Council where they, with the hands, decide the best course of action. Once you have served as Head of the Council you are removed from the raffle until the start of the next year.
History
Oroszko was founded by immigrants from Aviseau. When they arrived in Midovici its population was exploding, the influx of people from both across the ocean and elsewhere in Kinos flooded the city; to both ease the housing needed in Midovici and increase the amount of building materials Midovici offered money for anyone willing to set up a village nearby that could supply either wood or stone. The deal was popular and the Village of Oroszka was founded. Since its founding it has gained a reputation for the best place to trade for lumber, but even more it has acres of orchards that provide produce for many of the surrounding villages and Midovici itself. For these reasons and due to its proximity to Midovici it has quickly grown to a large village that promises continued growth.

Rumors, Legends, and Stories
Old Man Horn: Legend tells of a creature that walks the forest around Oroszko, Old Man Horn, with the body of a deer that walks on two legs, its arms ending in clawed hands rather than hooves and rows of sharp fangs protruding from its mouth. Where his eyes would be is nothing but empty sockets which hold white smoke that glows dimly, but more than this Old Man Horn has two large antlers sprouting from the top of their head that constantly drip blood. Old Man Horn hates any kind of light and hides constantly in the shadows, where, from the light of his eyes, only his face can dimly be seen. He is said to devour any he can, and takes a fondness in eating villagers from Oroszko, whom he has never forgiven for destroying so much of his forest. It is said an offering of a goat marked with the sign of Old Man Horn will placate the creature and keep you safe against his stalking.

Rumor has it that the Hands of the council have been making deals with Midovici, trying to bring a squad of Impures to be permanently based here to protect and help enforce the rulings of the the Council
