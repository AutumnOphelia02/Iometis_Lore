#Location 

Roke
Population: ~600
Demographics: 29% Hobgoblin, 20% Dwarf, 17% Orc, 15% Elf, 9% Kobold, 7% Tengu, 3% Other

# Overview
Roke is a small mining village that provides stone and ore for many of the nearby villages. Trading quarried stone and what ore they find for crops from [[Belhan]], lumber from [[Oroszko]], and meat and animal hides from [[Ish’conach]]. Like many settlements in the region it sends a tax of its goods to [[Midovici]] to ensure the larger city maintains and patrols the roads around it.

# Government
Roke is run by three druids, The Speaker of the Earth, Speaker of the Gardens, Speaker of the Beasts. Each druid handles their own domain, Speaker of the Earth is consulted before a new mine or quarry is started, Speaker of the beasts handles hunters and sanctions what time it is allowed to hunt. They also help with the raising of animals for work and slaughter. The Speaker of the Gardens is in charge of the farms, they aid when crops fail and sanction expansion of the farms. They are also consulted before areas of forest are clear cut for lumber or farmland. Mostly the three druids each independently deal with their own domains but they act together when crimes have been committed or when decisions for the whole town, such as trade agreements or the Midovici tax, must be made.

# History
Roke was formed from older tribes of people in the area, as more villages began to form Roke became the closest place where stone could be quarried, as they began to delve into the mountains and find ore as well as stone they quickly secured their position as the mining village in the surrounding area. The Druids of the old tribe maintain a balance with the natural world while still supplying the needs of Roke and the surrounding villages. As Roke became the mining village it is now, its population grew as people from surrounding villages and immigrants from Midovici found homes in this village.  

# Rumors, Legends, and Stories
Spirit Mist: Beware the mist that comes rolling down from the mountains as it is not truly mist, but rather a cacophony of spirits of all that died in the mountains without burial. Spirits of those exiled from the village for murder and other unsavory characters. Those who wander into the mist often get lost and are then subjected to the whims of the malicious spirits.

Rumor has it that Midovici has been asking for more ore in their tax, though rebuffed by the Speaker of the Earth they have threatened to trade with other people and stop patrols around Roke.

Rumor has it that a new resident is a Seer from Boromos who fled after her prophecy was deemed false, after being given the chance to recant or be exiled the Seer chose to stay true to her prophecy and was exiled.
