# Overview
Sobraca, the city built on an ancient ruin, home to the School of Arcane and Occult, The [[University of Arcane and Occult]], ever on the forefront of arcane knowledge and the ancient history of this land. What first began as a research facility delving into the secrets of the ruins the city has been built upon, has now become a large city in its own right. While not the largest city on Kinos, it is widely counted among the most powerful. Its highly defensive location, and the magical knowledge of its leaders and residents results in a city widely regarded with respect, especially after the Akenoan invasion roughly three centuries ago. 

# Government
Sobraca is run as a magocracy, a University of Magi lead by a Grandmagi run the city. The University of Magi are broken up into five main Colleges: Civics, Spellcraft, History, Artefacts, and Synthesis. Each school largely has total jurisdiction in regards to their purview. In matters pertaining to the creation of materials for expanding and maintaining the city the College of Synthesis typically has final say. While the Grandmagi can intervene in any of the Colleges business it is unusual to do so without cause.  

As the position of Grandmagi is a magocractic position anyone from the university, even a student, can challenge the Grandmagi to a Spellduel for the title of Grandmagi. Spellduels are unique events with conditions, rules, and arbitration changing duel to duel depending on the participants and the prize. Spellduels are rarely duels to the death, and indeed often do not even allow for casting of spells directly at or on the opponent, requiring demonstrations of magical prowess outside of combat.  

The Grandmagi, largely settles internal disputes between the colleges; allocation of University resources and instrument time, disputes of tasks that fall under the purview of multiple colleges, and other such things. As Grandmagi, one holds a lot of power but as anyone can challenge your position at anytime it is a difficult position to remain in without allies and a mastery of the magical arts.

# History
Sobraca started as a research facility on the ruins of [[Vresitur'alcenwrit'Jorentur'b'Andil]], a draconic and jotic city. The researchers delved deeper and deeper finding more and more artefacts, tomes and history. Slowly the needs of the research facility grew and a small town formed to supply those needs from the surrounding villages. Trading magic and artefacts for food and building supplies. This was the extent of the city until the Akenoan invasion three centuries ago.   

A militant city state from [[Akenenos Overview|Akenenos]] sailed south to Kinos and began conquering and claiming as much territory as they could. An unprepared and ununified Kinos as ripe for the plundering. When the Akenoans heard of a newly founded city on top of ancient ruins filled with magical treasures they thought it would be an easy victory. However Sobraca's magical knowledge and mountainous fortifications quickly proved more than a match for the invading forces. After a decisive victory at Sobraca the city sallied forth and drove the Akenoans from Kinos, solidifying themselves as a powerful city in the region and earning the respect, and perhaps a little fear, of their older counterparts.   

After this the small town exploded, with refugees of the invasion, those desiring to be trained in magical ways, or simply fascinated by the history of the draconic city came to the city in droves. Over a few decades the town quickly grew to what it now is. The researchers insisted that the magical knowledge they were discovering be shared and the University formed naturally from the desire to share that knowledge as well as train people to magically provide for the city and surrounding villages.
