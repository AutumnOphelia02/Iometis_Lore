An affordable inn located in The Greenway

This inn typically has a rust monster mascot but unfortunately the last one died and another is still being sourced. From the conversation with [[Maris]] and [[Celedwyn of Askia|Celedwyn]] it is understood that [[rust monsters]] are sourced for the inn by means of magical communication in which the creature is offered a supply of ferrous materials, and safe shelter in exchange for being put on display as a sort of mascot for the inn.
