The University of Arcane and Occult and the city of [[Sobraca]] are so tightly interwoven that distinguishing the two holds little real distinction.  

Admission to the University is a simple and informal matter: A prospective student will decide what courses they want to take and discussion directly with the professors of those classes. If there is enough room in the class they will be admitted and as a form of tuition they will be assigned a task roster. These tasks have two-fold purpose:  
1. To maintain and upkeep the school and city  
2. Serve as a 'practical class' on the various magical arts being taught in the class.  
With this form of tuition and practice, nearly every citizen in [[Sobraca]] has at least some degree of magical knowledge.

Rather than students taking several courses over the span of years culminating in a degree, the University awards Graduate Beads for each class a student has successfully passed. These beads are often worn or adorned on items to display to serve as a visible indication of their credentials.

The beads are made of different materials depending on both College and Marks earned in the class. With runes and symbols further clarifying the exact course the bead was earned in. Each College has three Marks of beads:  
**Passing marks** - indicating that the students demonstrated sufficient knowledge and skill to pass the course.  
**Top Marks** - indicating that the student demonstrated expert knowledge and skill and has mastered the topic of the course.  
**Distinction Marks** - Awarded to students that have demonstrated extreme mastery and understanding of the course. These marks are rarely awarded and are highly respected in the society of [[Sobraca]].
