Illusion Auditory
**Traditions** Arcane, Occult  
**Cast** 2 Actions (verbal, somatic)  
**Range** 30 feet; **Targets** 1 door, window, or other such portal  
**Duration** 10 minutes


---
This simple cantrip negates the sound of an opening and closing of a single portal (door window, gate, drawer, chest lid, or the like). Even the squeakiest door opens without a sound when under the effects of this spell. Silent portal covers only the normal means of opening and closing the targeted portal. Breaking a window or kicking in a door still makes noise as appropriate for the action, but opening a door that is loosely hanging by its hinges does not. Portals composed of magical energy are not affected by this spell. Portals under the effect of this spell do not allow for perception checks to hear them open. However if a creature can still see or otherwise sense the portal opening the may make a perception check as normal
