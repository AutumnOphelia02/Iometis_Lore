# Spells in Iometis
Spells are constantly evolving in the world, new spells are created and old ones rediscovered. Different mages with different needs will create or seek out different types of spells. There are many spells in the world of Iometis and many are not included in the rule books of Pathfinder.

# Creating New Spells
There are no formal rules for creating your own spells in the Core Rule Books of Pathfinder 2nd Edition. However they have rules for research and for learning new spells by combining these rules we can create our own rules for creating new spells
## Research
In order to create a new spell a character must spend some time researching the spell and developing it. A character needs to spend downtime doing research to complete the spell.  

A character can choose to do research by week or by day. This is a choice that will be made depending on the amount of time a character has to spend on creating the spell. Like crafting you do not need to finish research in a single go. For example, you may research for three days then spend some time adventuring before returning and spending 4 more days researching to finish up the first week of research.  

For each interval of research chose (week or day) the character must make a skill check, for the skill relevant to the tradition of the spell being created. The DC for this check is set by the GM and is usually a hard DC typical for the rank of the spell being developed.

You earn an amount of Research Points (RP) depending on the result of your check.  
**Critical Success:** You gain 2 RP  
**Success:** You gain 1 RP  
**Failure:** You make no progress  
**Critical Failure:** You make a critical mistake and lose 1 RP  

If you are working by week you need 1 RP per spell rank to complete research on a spell. If you are working per day you need 7 RP per spell rank to complete research on the spell. For every 7 days spent working on the research you must pay an amount equal to the cost of learning the spell of the same rank (See table 4-3 above).  

Once you have completed research on the spell you, your GM decides if it is a viable spell or not (see below). If it is a viable spell then you successfully learn the spell. If the spell is not a viable spell you and your GM can work to tweak it until it is viable.

## Viable Spells
A viable spell, simply put, is any spell the GM allows in their game. GM's should be careful allowing spells into their game, unlike magic items, cohorts, money, and other forms of rewards, it can be difficult to take away an overpowered spell once learned. For this reason it is important to work with your players to make sure the spell is of an appropriate power level.  

Comparing to published and offical spells are a good way to go and all newly created spells should be at least an uncommon rarity. A good rule of thumb is if a character would want to *always* prepare a spell it is probably over powered.  

As always mistakes can happen, it is okay to work with your players to tweak spells that have been created to make them less powerful or more powerful if the spell is too strong or weak. But you swill want to avoid taking away spells entirely unless everyone agrees that is the best course of action.
