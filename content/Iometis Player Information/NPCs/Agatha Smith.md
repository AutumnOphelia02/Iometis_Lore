#NPC
Agatha is the proprietor of Agatha's Smithy in [[Belhan]]

Description: Agatha is a short but broad woman (She/her), well built from her many years working the forge. She has tan skin and flaxen hair that has an increasing number of silver streaks running through it. She wear simple clothes, often prefering practicality over aesthetic. She is known for her good sense of humor and her raucous laugh, a generally mirthful person she is always happy and eager to make friends and share in a laugh or smile.
