#NPC

Annie Krey is a Orcish woman who works for [[Lillian Heart]]  as a bartender at the Weary Grey Stone tavern and inn in [[Belhan]].

Description: Annie is a thin but tall orcish woman (She/Her) who dresses in nice but practical clothing. She is renowed in Belhan for being a great listener in true bartender fashion. She has grey/green skin and long black hair often kept in ornate styles or braids, buns, and other styles. Annie is rather introverted, while sociable enough for her job she prefers to listen and interject with advice rather than be the prime subject of conversation.
