#NPC

# Description
"A well dressed goblin standing about 3'3" with dark olive green skin and bright red eyes. His waist coat, though once well kept now has tattered tails and mud along the edges, but his short top hat is pristinely kept on his bald head and his dark green bowtie is expertly tied"

A fairly prominent crime boss in [[Midovici]]. Has a strong foot hold in [[The Scrapes]] and is expanding his territory into the [[Trade Quarter]]. Is known to be ruthless, but he still works to maintain connections to his community.

# Locations
Operates primarily out of [[The Scrapes]] district in [[Midovici]]. Though is currently trying to expand foot hold in the [[Trade Quarter]].
