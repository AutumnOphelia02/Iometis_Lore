#NPC

Delbrin is the proprietor of Needle and Thread in [[Belhan]]

Description: Delbrin is a goblin person (They/them) with bright green skin and a receding black hair line. They wear spectacles and often dress in well made clothes a bit at odds with the village in which he lives. It is not unusual to see him dressed in a bright white shirt with a well made black jacket, vest, and pants. He is very quiet but when he speaks people tend to listen.
