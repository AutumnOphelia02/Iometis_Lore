*A small grey furred Ysoki dressed in durable work clothes under a thick leather apron. One might expect a manner of black smithing tools to adorn the apron and toolbelt wrapped around it. But instead strange bits and bobs can be seen. Vials of what appears to be animal manure, feathers, slime, and dusts are nestled between wands, rods, and other arcane tools.*

Hripti Woro is a Ysoki woman (she/her) who is the current Magi of Synthesis in [[Sobraca]].
