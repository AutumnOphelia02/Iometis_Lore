#NPC 
Jeren is the proprietor of Belhan Books in [[Belhan]]

Description: Jeren is a dwarven man (He/him) with shoulder length raven black hair and very fair skin. He is often seen around town with a small parisol to keep the sun off of him. He maintains a short beard and wears round spectacles. He is a very gregarious fellow and often seen at the Weary Greystone making new friends of the travelers and collecting their stories which, with their permission, he writes down in his own collection of stories.
