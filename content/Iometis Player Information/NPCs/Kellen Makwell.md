#NPC

Kellen is the proprietor of Kellen's General store in [[Belhan]]

Description: Kellen is a average sized human man (He/Him) with pale skin and chestnut brown hair kept short and often messy. He dresses in fine clothing, always keen to fit the latest fashion trends of Midovici, he is often seen at Needle and Thread creating custom outfits tailored to him. He is very outspoken and gregarious, always willing to chat with anyone who wants, and even those who don't sometimes.
