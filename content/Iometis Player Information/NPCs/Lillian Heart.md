#NPC

Lillian Heart is the Proprietor of the Weary Greystone Tavern and Inn in [[Belhan]]

Description: Lillian is a human woman (She/Her) with dark brown skin with thick curly black hair often tied back to keep out of her face while working. She dresses in simple but well made clothes often in cream colors with splashes of yellow and browns. She is very gregarious and sociable but more reserved than many other extroverts. Content to sit in silence or to loudly laugh in equal measure depending on the situation.

She employs one person [[Annie Krey]],  who acts as a bartender and kitchen aid while Lillian does most of the cooking and customer interactions.
