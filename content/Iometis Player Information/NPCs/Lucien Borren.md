#NPC
A male human in his late 40's, he is fair complected with short dark brown hair that is turning grey, he is clean shaven, and has an air of command about him, clearly he is used to getting his way through power or brute force.

Lucien was the captain of the guard in [[Ish’conach]] however, after the murder of [[Peckran Surx]], he returned to [[Midovici]] to report the crime and has since been under the employ of [[Leszek Ecskmanna]].
