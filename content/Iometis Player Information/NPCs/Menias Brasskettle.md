The first Tomori, created by [[Teliope Brasskettle]]. Menias is driven by curiosity, trying to discover who and what he is. While advanced in many regards he is still largely a child, needing to learn many things of the world, though his grasp of magic is advanced by any standards.

[[Greebo]] offered for Menias to learn, study, and be looked after at the chapel to [[Kohvaia]], as [[Teliope Brasskettle|Teliope]] seemed more than a bit overwhelmed with everything going on.
