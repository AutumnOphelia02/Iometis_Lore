Morag is a old student of the [[University of Arcane and Occult]], studying there roughly 40 years ago. He has recently returned to [[Sobraca]] from [[Midovici]] and challenged [[Teliope Brasskettle]] to a spellduel for the title of Grandmagi. A spellduel of demonstration of magical power over life. His ritual of lichdom was victor over [[Teliope Brasskettle|Teliope's]] creation of a [[Tomori]]. With this duel he has cemented himself as the new Grandmagi of [[Sobraca]] and seems to be acclimating well to his new position, and his new existence as a lich, the first and currently sole lich known.
