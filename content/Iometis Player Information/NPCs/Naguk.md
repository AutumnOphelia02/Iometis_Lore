#NPC
# Description
*A simply dressed orcish woman towering at a height of 6'11". She has dark forest green skin and eyes, with carefully kept coarse black hair. Her clothes, while simple, are clean and well kept.*

A fairly prominent crime boss in [[Midovici]]. She has a strong foot hold in [[The Puddles]] and is attempting to expand her territory into the [[Trade Quarter]]. She is known to treat her people with respect and benevolence but still runs a fairly ruthless crime organization. Currently is at odds with [[Ssevsk]], having more resources available to expand into [[Trade Quarter]] but lacking the adjacent territory to move through. The two are in a tense battle for control of various sections of the [[Trade Quarter]] and [[The Puddles]]

# Locations
Operates primarily out of [[The Puddles]] district in [[Midovici]]. Though is currently trying to expand foot hold in the [[Trade Quarter]].
