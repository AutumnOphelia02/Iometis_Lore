#Dead 

Died on the 9th of Octhal in the Year 1518 PWC

Before his death, he was the self imposed leader of [[Ish’conach]]. Owning much of the land and lakeside fisheries. In the year 1518 PWC he began to employ a necromancer who raised mindless undead minions to work the fisheries. This greatly upset the populace of [[Ish’conach]], and eventually led to his death.
