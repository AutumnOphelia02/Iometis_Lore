#NPC

The Current Maven of [[Belhan]].

Description: Runlain is a tall and thin dwarven woman (She/her). Her face is marked with laugh lines and her hair has long been silver from age, but despite this she is still very active. She often keeps her hair in two ornate braids that frame her face with carved bark clasps holding them tied. She dresses in simple work clothes with little evidence of her station. She is known to be very reserved but unfailingly kind. She is a favorite of the village children whom she loves to entertain with her magic when she has the free time. In her rulings she is very firm, rarely swaying once a descision has been made, however she often takes a long time and council with other people before coming to any decision.
