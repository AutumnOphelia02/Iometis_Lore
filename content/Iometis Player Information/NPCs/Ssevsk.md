# Description
"A dark purple scaled lizardfolk dressed in patchwork armor stands around 6' tall. Their body is covered in scars that are prominently displayed somtimes at the sacrifice of pieces of armor."

A fairly prominent crime boss in [[Midovici]]. Has a strong foot hold in [[The Puddles]] and is trying to expand his territory into the [[Trade Quarter]]. Currently is at odds with [[Naguk]], having more territory closer to the [[Trade Quarter]] but less resources to expand into the district the two are in a tense battle for territory and control over various areas of the [[Trade Quarter]].

# Locations
Operates primarily out of [[The Puddles]] district in [[Midovici]]. Though is currently trying to expand foot hold in the [[Trade Quarter]].
