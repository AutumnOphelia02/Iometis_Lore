*An old gnomish woman dressed in a fine blue and purple robe. Her bright silver hair kept neatly in a bun allowing her intricately pierced ears to be on display. She carries herself with an air of confidence that is undercut by a heavy layer of fatigue. Adorning her robes are many pockets filled with wands, crystals, and many other arcane accoutrement.*

Teliope is a old gnomish woman (She/Her) who has held the position of Grandmagi of [[Sobraca]] for the past 89 years and before that the position of Magi of Spellcraft since the schools creation 344 years ago. Indeed she was one of the original researchers that came to [[Sobraca]] to delve into the Ruins of [[Vresitur'alcenwrit'Jorentur'b'Andil]]. Most of her research has been on ancient draconic magic and in particular teleportation magic. She discovered how to teleport and was a leading mind on the development of permanent teleportation circles. Renowned throughout the world for these feats allowing dramatic travel and interplanar travel to take place. For the past three decades however she has put out little research other than some passing assistance on other wizards papers. Some believe she is growing tired of academia and will likely retire soon, while other believe she has been working on something and doesn't want anyone stealing her research out from under her. Regardless of which is true, many see has been resting on her laurels for too long, and there are many rumours at the university of various professors, Magi, and even students who are testing the waters to see if they could up seat the long standing Grandmagi.

However it was a relative new comer who finally did exactly that. [[Morag Dro'Gura]] challenged Teliope to a spell duel, demonstrating their magical mastery of life. Teliope created the first [[Tomori]], [[Menias Brasskettle]], while [[Morag Dro'Gura|Morag]] performed the ritual of Lichdom granting him functional immortality. The upseated Teliope has since been in contact with the group to try and figure out who [[Morag Dro'Gura|Morag]] is, why he wanted the position of Grandmagi, and what it means for [[Sobraca]].
