# Description
"A scrawny looking elven woman with a myriad of scars crossing her body and a sharp rat like face. She dresses in well worn clothes of practical nature and dark color. Her blonde hair is kept shaven close to her head and she stands roughly 5'10" though often appears shorter due to her slouched nature"

A fairly prominent crime boss in [[Midovici]]. Has a foot hold in [[The Scrapes]]. 
# Locations
Operates primarily out of [[The Scrapes]] district in [[Midovici]].
