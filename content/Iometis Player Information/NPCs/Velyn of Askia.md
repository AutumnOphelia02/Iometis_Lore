*A short elven woman dressed in earth tones, a green shirt, brown trousers and cardigan, with her honey colored hair carefully braided and kept out of her field of view. She carries a simple staff adorned with many trinkets, fine filigreed sigils, small crystals, and metal spheres. Despite her young age she wears an impressive amount of [[University of Arcane and Occult|Graduate Beads]], all notably gemstone or gold.*

Velyn is a young elven woman (She/her) who is the current Magi of Spellcraft in [[Sobraca]].
