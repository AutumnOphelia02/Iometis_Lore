# Creating a Place in Iometis
Iometis is large, and any character you create can be from anywhere in the world. I have created several continents and island nations but beyond the [[Kinoan Island Overview|Kinoan Islands]] little is established. That is to say have fun! This world is your playground and I encourage you creating as much or as little as you desire. Want to create a whole new city, nation, continent, planet? Go for it! With as much or as little detail as you would like. (I would appreciate adherence to the core elements of what has already been created, but again please take what is there and run with it). But whatever your characters origin, please create some reason for them to be in [[Kinos Overview|Kinos]] when the campaign begins.
# Starting Region
We will be starting the campaign in the [[Kinoan Island Overview|Kinoan Islands]]. More Specifically [[Kinos Overview|Kinos]], and even more specifically the region around [[Midovici]]. I would like to begin the story in or around one of the smaller settlements around [[Midovici]], that is [[Oroszko]], [[Ish’conach]], [[Belhan]], or [[Roke]]. But depending on the characters everyone creates and the stories they want to tell with them we can begin anywhere that makes the most sense. 
# Story Elements
I currently have a loose collection of story hooks to start the campaign, however I really want to tailor these to the characters you will create. When creating a character think of loose motivations, goals, and desires they might have so that we can work together to create a story we are all interested in seeing unfold. You can create as lengthy of a backstory as you should like and I will adapt the story to try to best synthesize all of the characters backstories together to create a fun campaign. I will do my best to work with you as the story unfolds to ensure everyone is happy and satisfied with it.
Without spoiling anything some of the hook themes include:  
	1. A Brewing War - Rumour has it that [[Midovici]] is preparing for war, but against who and why is the larger question.  
	2. Fae Intrigue - The courts of the Fae ever have their fingers in mortal pies. It seems that some of the maneuverings of Kinoan politics is being influenced in some way by plots and power of the Fae.   
	3. Corrupt Political Machinations - [[Midovici]] is having growing pains, under the population growth from refugees from both [[Zamonia Overview|Zamonia]] and [[Cetel Overview|Cetel]] the city is being strained for both room and resources, and it seems the members of the [[Diamond Circle]] are spread too thin, or simply uninterested in anything other the growth of their own political power.  

If any of these appeal to you and the character you want to create please let me know and I we can work together to create the character you want with all the information they should have.

# Gathering the Party
In an attempt to avoid the "Meet at a Tavern" trope I am going to attempt to have the characters meet organically, in this way the first session will likely have the characters in relatively disparate locations, the players will take turns in the spotlight until the party eventually all gathers together. This is to say if you want, in the first session to have any vignettes of your backstory narrated or played through we can work together to make that happen. 

# Rarity Traits
When creating your character please limit yourself to spells, items, feats, and other such things of common rarity. If you would like to utilize something of a less common rarity please talk with me about it and we can discuss how to best implement it, if we can. The exception to this is ancestries, feel free to create a character of any ancestry of common or uncommon. Rare ancestries are available but I would like to discuss those on a case by case basis.

# Magic
Magic is still being developed in this world no spells of rank 8 or higher have yet been discovered or developed. Currently only a handful of rank 7 spells have been discovered or developed and very few casters can even cast 6th or 7th rank spells. While this will mostly be a world building and lore relevant piece of information if we get to high enough levels for it to affect player choices we will get into more details.  
Please see the Magic of Iometis folder for more information about [[Creating New Spells]], and homebrew spells in the world of Iometis. I will add more spells to this section of this site as they become relevant and available to the party.
