Aratea is the deity of the Silver Moon, champion of freedom and individuality. Those who value freedom, oppose oppression and see the merits in everyone's uniqueness often find themselves drawn to Aratea. Aratea is known as the Silver Moon as they were born from the larger of two moons that orbit Iometis and her symbol is a vertical line of moons showing it’s different phases.

Edicts: Free all who suffer under oppression, help people to find and express their individuality, pursue your own freedom.

Anathema: enslave or oppress others, conformity, enforce your will on others 

Areas of Concern: The Moon, Freedom, Individuality

Follower Alignments: NG, CG, CN
