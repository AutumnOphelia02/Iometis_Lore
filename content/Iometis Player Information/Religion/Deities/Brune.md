Brune is the goddess of familial bonds, a fierce protector and traditionalist Brunes worship steeped in ancient rites. She rewards those who protect and defend the innocent and those who adhere to traditions of her faith, these traditions are held in highest respect in her church. She is patron to all families, be they of blood or choice, and her fiercest wrath is saved for those who abuse or mistreat the people of their family. She is known as the Warding Hearth and her symbol is a blazing hearth whose flames are in the shape of a shield.

Edicts: Observe and respect the traditions of the faith, protect and defend the innocent and vulnerable, Ensure the safety and well-being of your family

Anathema: Harm the innocent, break traditions or vows, harm or abandon those in your care

Areas of Concern: Family, Protection, Tradition

Follower Alignments: NG, LG, LN
