Dusrael is the deity of travel, messengers, and curiosity, patron to those who walk and forge the roads of the wide world. Explorers driven by curiosity, messengers who spend their days on the roads of the world, and any who hear the call of the wind and road can find solace and safety from Dusrael. They are known as The Dusted Traveler and, somehow, seem to be able to pierce the divine gate and walk the world of Iometis, appearing as a dust covered traveler offering aid to fellow wanders. Their symbol is a road disappearing to the horizon.

Edicts: Follow your curiosity and wander wide, travel the world and see its wonders, forge new roads so others can travel them too

Anathema: Remain in a single place for too long, ignore your curiosity, hide, remove, or obscure paths or roads so others cannot walk them.

Areas of Concern: Travel, Curiosity, Messengers

Follower Alignments: NG, N, CN
