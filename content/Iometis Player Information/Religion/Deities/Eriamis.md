Eriamis is the patron deity of storms and celebration, encouraging all to drink deeply from the cup of revelry and enjoy the many pleasures of this world. To let the rain kiss their naked skin, to feel rumble of thunder and laughter deep in their bellies, and revel in the flashing crack of lightning and a well played trick. The fluid nature of Eriamis bleeds into their faith and those who follow rarely do so in the same way creating a diverse church with many unique practices. Their symbol is a mask of clouds with a wide grin of arcing lightning.

Edicts: Revel in the pleasures of this world and follow your passions, bring mirth to everyone you can, honor and respect storms for both their beauty and their destruction

Anathema: disrupt or end a celebration before its natural conclusion, use pleasure as an excuse to hurt or abuse someone, waste alcohol or other drugs, refuse a genuine invitation to a revelry. 

Areas of Concern: Storms, Revelry, Pleasure

Follower Alignments: N, CN, CG
