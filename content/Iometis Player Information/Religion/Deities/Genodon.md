Genodon is patron to those who record history, both mundane and grandiose, and those who strengthen and expand civilization. They reward those who expand a civilization's infrastructure, improve its ability to sustain and grow its population, as well as those who truthfully record and pass on history. Genodon is known as the Clockwork Architect and the symbol of their faith is a tall clock tower in the center of three converging roads.

Edicts: Expand civilization for the improvement of its people, record truthful history and learn from it, collaborate and learn from other civilizations

Anathema: Colonize land that already has people present, let nature reclaim land, alter or change truthful history

Areas of Concern: Civilization, History, Technology

Follower Alignments: LN, N, LG
