Ido the Divine Muse, patron of creativity, music, and love. Ido is worshiped by those who see the magic in music and art and those who devote themselves to spreading love and kindness to the world. He rewards those who creatively bring arts to life and those who seek and give true love, be it romantic, familial, or platonic. Ido’s symbol is a golden lyre whose curving sides look like two lovers just before embracing.

Edicts: Utilize your creativity to create art in any form, spread love to all you can, Seek out art of all forms to appreciate and enjoy

Anathema: Destroy art, spreading or allowing the spread of hateful or harmful ideologies, censorship of art

Areas of Concern: Music, Love, Arts

Follower Alignments: NG, CG, CN
