Jalowei is patron to healers, academics, and those who seek the truth. They reward those who seek knowledge for the betterment of others and those who tend to the wounded, ill, and diseased. Jalowei’s key tenet is that knowledge, truth, and healing are rights of all people and should be given freely to any who need. They are known as Sage Owl as their symbol is an owl with outstretched wings with sage and yarrow clutched in its talons.

Edicts: Heal those who you can, Teach those you can, seek and teach the truth

Anathema: Withhold care from a patient, intentionally spread misinformation or falsehoods, to prohibit people from obtaining education

Areas of Concern: Healing, Truth, Learning

Follower Alignments: NG, N, CG, LG
