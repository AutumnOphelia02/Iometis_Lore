Kohvaia is the deity of death, the afterlife, and fate. When a creature dies it has a series of choices to make about how their soul will move on and it is Kohvaia who helps guide them through these decisions. Those who worship Kohvaia often act to perform burial rites, help the bereaved, protect against desecration of the dead, though many also act as healers, understanding that they will fail when a person's time is up, but that is for Kohvaia and not them to decide. Kohvaia is known as The Grey Moon as the smaller moon that orbits Iometis is their domain, it is said that all souls must pass through the moon on their way to the afterlife. Their symbol is a robed figure standing on a crescent moon against a starless sky.

Edicts: All that live must one day die, that which is fated must come to pass, the dead deserve respect and proper treatment

Anathema: tampering with a souls natural course after death, denying a person choice or autonomy, desecration of a body or resting place

Areas of Concern: Death, Fate, Choice

Follower Alignments: N, CN, LN
