Kygin, the Risen Devil, in his birth was born of the Infernal Maeserai but saw the pain and damage he caused and chose a path of redemption. Now he stands proud among the celestial Maeserai, divine proof that no one is beyond redemption. He champions justice for all people and rewards those who fight against injustice, and offer redemption to those who truly seek it. His symbol is a horned devil with white feathered wings soaring upward with a brandished spear.

Edicts: Fight for justice for all people, Give everyone a chance of redemption, honor the rights of all people and fight to defend them

Anathema: Prevent someone the opportunity for redemption, stand idle while injustices are enacted, honor or serve unjust systems or people

Areas of Concern: Justice, Redemption, Honor

Follower Alignments: LG, NG, LN
