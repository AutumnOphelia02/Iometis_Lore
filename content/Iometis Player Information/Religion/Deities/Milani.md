Milani is the patron mother of those who war against oppression, rewarding those willing to sacrifice their lives and use whatever tools are available to fight for those who cannot defend themselves, especially people who have been captured or enslaved. Throughout Iometis, Milani is also known as the Everbloom, as the symbol of her church is a beautiful rose growing from blood-soaked soil.

Edicts: confront oppression in all its forms, defend the common folk, overcome despair to seize victory

Anathema: abandon those in need, enslave or oppress others, harm the innocent through direct or inadvertent action

Areas of Concern: devotion, hope, and uprisings

Follower Alignments: NG, CG, CN
