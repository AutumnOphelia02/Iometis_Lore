Ni’eresh is patron to those who revel in tricks, pranks, and new experiences. They reward those who upset the status quo, make others laugh, and seek out novelty in life. While they espouse trickery and pranks they should always serve a purpose, to humble the proud or bring joy to the downtrodden. Tricks that only harm or humiliate are frowned upon as crass mockeries of true trickery. They are known as The Morphic Harlequin and their symbol is a red and gold jester's cap ringed with three juggled balls of fire.

Edicts: Trick and prank those you can, seek out new experiences, bring laughter to yourself and others 

Anathema: Use a trick to harm the innocent, to adhere to a strict routine, to intentionally disrupt or end laughter or mirth.  

Areas of Concern: Trickery, Laughter, and Novelty

Follower Alignments: CG, CN, N
