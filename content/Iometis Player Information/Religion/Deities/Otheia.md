Otheia is the patron of agriculture, harvest and brewing. They reward those who tend to cultivated lands and those who harvest the fruits of agriculture. Otheia was the first to brew alcohol and its creation is still their domain and many brewmasters pay their respects to Otheia for inspiration and successful brews. They are known as The Amber Reaper and their symbol is a sickle hanging over a full cornucopia.

Edicts: Maintain healthy crops and livestock, Share harvested food with the whole community, Brew alcohol 

Anathema: Destroy farmland, Waste crops, livestock, or alcohol, Hoard a harvest 

Areas of Concern: Agriculture, Harvest, Brewing

Follower Alignments: CG, NG, N
