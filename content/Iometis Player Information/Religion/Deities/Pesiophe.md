Pesiophe is the goddess of physical prowess, valor, and bravery, she is patron to many sports especially those of martial skill such as fencing, jousting, and archery. She rewards those who seek physical challenges, showcase their bravery, and can prove their valor in battle. While condemning violence without cause, she condones violence in pursuit of justice and freedom. When there is no just cause for battle she directs her followers to prove their might in consensual, and non deadly, contests and duels. She is known as The Souls Crucible and her symbol is a hammer striking down on a round shield.

Edicts: Never shrink down from a challenge, prove your martial skill in just combat, grant everyone fair combat

Anathema: Refuse a challenge of physical prowess, flee from combat, attempt to sway or change the outcome of a fair fight 

Areas of Concern: Valor, Bravery, and Physical Prowess

Follower Alignments: LN, LG, N
