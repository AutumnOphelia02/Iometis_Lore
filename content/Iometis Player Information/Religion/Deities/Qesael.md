Qesael deity born of stars and void, patron of knowledge, prophecy, and magic. They reward those who revere the stars, those who wish to expand their knowledge, learn magic, and oracles who predict the future. They are known as the Astral Weaver, and are known to spell secrets of the future in the stars to those who know how to read them. Their symbol is black robed figure with outstretched hands, between their hands and in the deep cowl of their cloak are innumerable stars.

Edicts: Seek knowledge, learn and practice magic, predict and inform on the future

Anathema: Destroy knowledge, destroy magic, ignore or deny a prophecy

Areas of Concern: Stars, Knowledge, prophecy, and Magic

Follower Alignments: NG,N, CN
