Rone, twin of Wren, rarely seen separately, deity of nature, plants, and death. They reward those who act in one with nature, especially those who revere and respect the flora of the world, in all its kinds. They are the embodiment of change and the later half of the cycle of life. Rone is the deity of death as a necessary part of life. They are often worshiped in conjunction with Wren who represents the other half of the cycle of life. They are known as Old Man Mushroom and their symbol is a deer skull with a multitude of fungi growing off of it.

Edicts: Respect and preserve nature, respect death as the natural flow of life, do not let a death go to waste

Anathema: destroy or despoil nature, interfere with the natural course of death, prevent the dead from returning back to the soil and earth 

Areas of Concern: Nature, Plants, Death

Follower Alignments: CN, N, NG
