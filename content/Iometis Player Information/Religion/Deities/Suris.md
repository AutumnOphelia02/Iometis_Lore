Suris, the goddess of the sun, creation, and the forge. She is patron to craftspeople, especially those who work with metal, and those who revere the sun. She rewards those who create and craft new and artisanal items, and is particularly fond of those who create items that benefit society and support the community. She revels in the blending of art and functionality, and her worshipers often create some of the most beautiful items while still maintaining their functionality. She is known as The Smith of Souls and her symbol is a hammer striking a glowing anvil with a rising sun behind it.

Edicts: Create items for the betterment of society, Create beauty as much as functionality, Respect and honor the sun

Anathema: Destroy tools of creation, destroy creations that aid society, intentionally create poor quality items 

Areas of Concern: Sun, Creation, Forge

Follower Alignments: LG, NG, CG, N
