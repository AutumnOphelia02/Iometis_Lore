Valashrota is the god of secrets, dreams and memories. He is patron to those who seek and keep secrets, interpret and read dreams, and record memories. To know a secret is sacred to the worshipers of Valashrota and often will never be shared but must be discovered anew. Memory Keepers are special members of his clergy that record and protect the memories of his worshipers, though these memories are kept a secret to all except the Keepers and the person who they came from. He is known as The Whispered Dream and his symbol is a brain with secret symbols and lines drawn over it.

Edicts: Find and keep secrets, respect and remember dreams, record and protect memories

Anathema: Share a secret that isn’t yours to share, alter or destroy someone's memories, dismiss or ignore dreams

Areas of Concern: Secrets, Dreams, Memories

Follower Alignments: N, CN, CG
