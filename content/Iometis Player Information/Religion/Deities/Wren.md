Wren, twin of Rone and rarely seen without them, deity of nature, animals, and birth. They reward those who act in one with nature, especially those who revere and respect the animals and fauna of the world. They are the embodiment of life and the first part of the cycle of life. Wren is the deity of birth as the beginning of the endless cycle of life, life is born anew to age and eventually die to feed new life. They are known as the Raven Stag and their symbol is a mother deer laying next to its young faun.

Edicts: Aid in the healthy birth of all creatures, protect and preserve nature, protect the natural cycle of life

Anathema: Hunting animals for sport, force a birth that is harmful or dangerous to the birther or child, destroy or despoil nature

Areas of Concern: Nature, Animals, Birth

Follower Alignments: CN, N, NG
