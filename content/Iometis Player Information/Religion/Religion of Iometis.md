There are many deities in the World of Iometis, many different divine beings worshiped by many. There are the Elemental Colossi which created the world and the cosmos in a time before the Orrery was created. There are Fae Nobles, older than even the Colossi with their own ancient and mysterious world. Beyond the Orrery in the great dark expanse of the planescape lies the Elder Ones, mysterious creatures of great power, deific power, whose true intentions are unknown. But few of these beings have a religious following outside of secret cult worship or individual prayers. The gods worshiped by most of the populace, particularly in Kinos are of the Maeserai, the beings created in the destruction of the Great Wheel and creation of the Orrery, these beings created most of the creatures and ancestries that reside on Iometis and the planes beyond. Of the Maeserai it is the Good and Neutral beings that are commonly worshiped with those of an Evil alignment again often relegated to secretive cult worship.
