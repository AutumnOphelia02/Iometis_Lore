Petitioners are the reconstituted souls of the dead. Their afterlives vastly dependent on their lived life, the deity they worshipped (if any), and the manner of their death.

All petitioners are first reconstituted in [[Lokan]]. Here they are bound until bodies are given their proper burials; what that entails depends on the rites of their Deity, or their own personal beliefs. Those who do not receive a proper burial become trapped in [[Lokan]]. Those souls [[Lokan]] have three choices: They can remain in [[Lokan]] hoping that their body receives proper burial, they can drink the draught of souls beyond which no one knows what lies, or they can choose to bathe in the Creation Well being reborn into the world as a duskwalker. Certain benevolent deities, allow the souls given proper burial the option to be reborn or drink the draught of souls, but many selfish ones keep the souls they have as tools or entertainment. Some souls remain trapped in [[Lokan]] even after given proper burial if the burial rites don't match their chosen rites. Any involved deities thus debating and fighting over the destination of the soul. Often between benevolent deities the soul in question is given consideration and choice in the matter, however when an selfish deity has a right to a soul they often will fight to keep it, either out of a sick pleasure to cause pain to the soul, or as a desire to gain more souls for their own use and purpose.

In general, petitioners appear in the form that they had before they died, though they may be remade by the planes or the powers of those planes to fit the nature of their particular afterlife. 

Petitioners can be found in almost any transcendental plane, depending on the belief's of the soul, though they can end up in other planes. The Ethereal plane can become a horrid limbo, trapping petitioners whose souls fail to be carried away from the Telluric Plane or those who try to escape [[Lokan]] and make their way back to the material plane.
