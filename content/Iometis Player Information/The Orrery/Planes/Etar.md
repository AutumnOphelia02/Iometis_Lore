# Links

## Coterminous
Etoc is Coterminous for 3 days every 7 years

When Etar is Ascendant and coterminous heat becomes a constant problem. Fire's burn hotter, and even the sun seems to scorch more intensely.

Spells with the fire trait or that manipulate, use, or create fire are cast as if they were heighted by 1 level. Any summon spell that summons a creature with the fire Trait (or that logically comes from Etar) may be cast as if it were heightened by one level, but only if summoning an fire creature.

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Etar may roll twice on their ritual check and take the higher result.

## Remote
Etoc is Remote for 3 days every 7 years

When Etul is Descendant and remote fires are cooler and harder to keep alive. The sun is more mild and temperatures in general are cooler.

Spells with the fire trait or that manipulate, use, or create fire are impeded. Also it is harder to summon natives of the plane of Water, any summon spell that summons a creature with the fire trait (or that logically comes from Etar) is cast as if it were 1 spell level lower than it actually is, a 1st level summon spell fails all together.

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Etar must roll twice on their ritual check and take the lower result.
