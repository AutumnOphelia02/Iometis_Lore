# Links

## Coterminous
Etoc is Coterminous for five days every 11.67 years

When Etoc is Ascendant and coterminous earth and stone becomes more stable, harder to dig and mine through. Seams of raw minerals and gems will grow in the deep areas of the earth. Gravity grows heavier the deeper you go.

Stone objects and buildings gain +2 hardness and +5 hit points/inch of thickness. Spells with the earth trait or that manipulate, use, or create earth, stone, or mud are cast as if they were heighted by 1 level. Any summon spell that summons a creature with the Earth Trait (or that logically comes from Etoc) may be cast as if it were heightened by one level, but only if summoning an Earth creature.

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Etoc may roll twice on their ritual check and take the higher result.

## Remote
Etoc is Remote for five days every 11.67 years

When Etoc is Descendant and remote earth and stone becomes less stable, easier dig and mine through and landslides, earthquakes, and rockfalls become more common.

Stone objects and buildings gain -2 hardness and -5 hit points/inch of thickness. Spells with the earth trait or that manipulate, use, or create earth, stone, or mud are impeded. Also it is harder to summon natives of the plane of Earth, any summon spell that summons a creature with the earth trait (or that logically comes from Etoc) is cast as if it were 1 spell level lower than it actually is, a 1st level summon spell fails all together.

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Etoc must roll twice on their ritual check and take the lower result.
