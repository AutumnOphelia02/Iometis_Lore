# Links

## Coterminous
Etoc is Coterminous for 4 days every 9.33 years

When Etul is Ascendant and coterminous rain constantly falls, the seas are tumultuous and dangerous with water levels rising dramatically.

Spells with the water trait or that manipulate, use, or create water are cast as if they were heighted by 1 level. Any summon spell that summons a creature with the Water Trait (or that logically comes from Etul) may be cast as if it were heightened by one level, but only if summoning an Water creature.

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Etul may roll twice on their ritual check and take the higher result.

## Remote
Etoc is Remote for 4 days every 9.33 years

When Etul is Descendant and remote rain never falls, oceans and seas calm to near still perfection and drop in water level.

Spells with the water trait or that manipulate, use, or create water are impeded. Also it is harder to summon natives of the plane of Water, any summon spell that summons a creature with the water trait (or that logically comes from Etul) is cast as if it were 1 spell level lower than it actually is, a 1st level summon spell fails all together.

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Etul must roll twice on their ritual check and take the lower result.
