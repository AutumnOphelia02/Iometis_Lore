It is the land of grey mist  
It is Void and Vitality balanced  
It is a gateway, liminality of life  

Lokan is the realm of the dead, created by the coalescence of the many deaths that occurred in the shattering of the great wheel and the arcane ritual that destroyed it. It is a vast plane with many regions and domains. The most common region for planar travelers from the material plane is that under [[Kohvaia|Kohvaia's]] influence. To visitors, this plane is a vast grey landscape, with muted colors and thick swirling mists obscuring much, however the denizens of this plane, mostly [[Petitioner's]], report that this plane is as vivid and diverse as the material plane.  

[[Kohvaia]] has a strong reverence for the souls that they shepherd into the afterlife, they despise tampering with a souls own choice and autonomy, but also understand that death is a natural part of every life. The souls of those created by the Maeserai all pass through Lokan upon their death as [[Petitioner's]] where they go from there depends on the manner of their death and burial, giving different Maeserai claim to the soul. [[Kohvaia]] alone acts as the final arbiter of these disputes, and every soul is given the choice to drink the draught of souls.  




# Links
## Coterminous: 
Lokan becomes coterminous for 35 days every 94.8years  

When Lokan is coterminous the pathways from Elderest to this realm are shorter and easier to navigate. Lost spirits find their way back to Elderest becoming ghosts or worse. Ghosts and visions of the deceased are common but still unsettling and dangerous.   

Spells that affect deceased souls, notably resurrection magic, are more potent and its easier for souls to cross back over. A caster attempting a resurrect ritual receives a +10 circumstance bonus to succeed on the resurrection ritual. However, it is easier for unintended souls to slip through with or instead of the intended target. No matter the result of the resurrection ritual check the GM secretly rolls on the following table.

## Remote: 
Lokan becomes remote for 35 days every 94.8years  

When Lokan is remote the pathways from Elderest to Lokan are long and difficult to navigate. Without a divine guide it is nearly impossible for spirits to find their way out of the material plane to Lokan or vice versa.   

Resurrection spells are much more difficult to succeed during this time resurrection ritual DC’s are all increased by +10. This includes the DC’s of secondary caster's checks. Even if these difficulties are overcome there is a 50% chance (01-50 on percentile die) that even with a successful check the spell fails. Once a resurrection ritual has failed it cannot be attempted again for the same soul.
