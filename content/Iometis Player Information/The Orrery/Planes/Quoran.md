# Links

## Coterminous
Quoran is Coterminous for 3 days every 7 years always occurring on the winter solstice and the two days surrounding it.  

When Quoran is Ascendant and coterminous the air temperature drops dramatically making it dangerous to go outside unprotected from the chill. Blizzards and lightning storms are common often occurring at the same time. Ice and snow are more solid and do not melt as quickly even when exposed to direct flame. Ice gains +5 hardness and +5 hp/inch of thickness.  

Spells with the cold or lightning trait or that manipulate, use, or create snow, ice, or lightning are cast as if they were heighted by 1 level. Any summon spell that summons a creature with the cold or lightning Trait (or that logically comes from Quoran) may be cast as if it were heightened by one level, but only if summoning an cold or lighting creature.  

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Quoran may roll twice on their ritual check and take the higher result.  

## Remote
Etoc is Remote for 3 days every 7 years, always occurring on the summer solstice and the two days surrounding it.  

When Quoran is Descendant and remote everything is warmer, snow never falls and storm clouds never form, the colder regions of Iometis grow warmer and ice and snow have trouble staying in solid form.

Spells with the cold or lightning trait or that manipulate, use, or create ice, snow, or lighting are impeded. Also it is harder to summon natives of the plane of Ice and Storms, any summon spell that summons a creature with the Cold or Lightning trait (or that logically comes from Quoran) is cast as if it were 1 spell level lower than it actually is, a 1st level summon spell fails all together.  

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Quoran must roll twice on their ritual check and take the lower result.
