# Links

## Coterminous

Rathlios is coterminous for 94 days (4 months or 1 season) every 100.25 years. Meaning it rotates 1 season each cycle  

Spells with the wood or plant trait or that manipulate, use, or create wood or plants are cast as if they were heighted by 1 level. Any summon spell that summons a creature with the wood, plant, or fae Trait (or that logically comes from Rathlios) may be cast as if it were heightened by one level, but only if summoning an fae creature.  

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Rathlios may roll twice on their ritual check and take the higher result.  

## Remote

Rathlios is remote for 94 days (4 months or 1 season) every 100.25 years. Meaning it rotates 1 season each cycle. 

Spells with the wood or plant trait or that manipulate, use, or create wood or plants are impeded. Any summon spell that summons a creature with the wood, plant, or fae Trait (or that logically comes from Rathlios) are cast as if it were 1 spell level lower than it actually is, a 1st level summon spell fails all together.  

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Rathlios must roll twice on their ritual check and take the lower result.
