# Links

## Coterminous

Rathlios is coterminous for 94 days (4 months or 1 season) every 100.25 years. Meaning it rotates 1 season each cycle  

Spells with the wood or plant trait or that manipulate, use, or create water are cast as if they were heighted by 1 level. Any summon spell that summons a creature with the Water Trait (or that logically comes from Etul) may be cast as if it were heightened by one level, but only if summoning an Water creature.  

In addition anyone participating in a Planar Binding ritual that is binding a creature from Etul may roll twice on their ritual check and take the higher result.
