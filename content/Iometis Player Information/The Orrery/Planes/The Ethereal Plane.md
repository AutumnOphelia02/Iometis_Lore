The Hidden Mists  
The Plane of Ghosts  
The Eternal Wander  

The Ethereal plane is a plane out of phase with the material plane. A coterminous plane with the Telluric Plane. It is a misty, fog-bound dimension. Those who have been there describe it as a collection of swirling mists and colorful fogs. The material plane is visible from the Ethereal plane but it appears muted and indistinct; its colors blurring into each other and its edges turning fuzzy. Those on the Ethereal plane watch the material plane as though viewing it through distorted or frosted glass.
