It is nature unbound.

It is as wild as the wind.

It is gentle as a lamb and protective as a lion.

The Spirit World is a Plane of brilliance and wonder, a realm of extreme sensation and lucid reality. Colors are brighter, sounds more distinct, and every sense is more keenly aware of its surroundings. Travelers can often be overwhelmed by the overstimulation of the plane if they travel there unprepared. The Spirit World is coexistent with the Material plane, Certain areas of the Spirit World are closely tied to Elderest and the landscape of these regions closely conform to that on the material plane, however travelling away from these areas may lead to places with no relation to their counterpart in the material plane. Wild and bizarre natural features are common, and the spirit protectors of the natural world call this plane home only pushing through to the material world when something threatens the land in their charge.
