# Welcome  
Welcome all to Iometis! An ancient world being discovered by young nations and peoples. This site will keep track of many lore documents and notes for you all to reference at your leisure. If you have any requests, comments, or concerns. Please let me know!  

The most relevant pages are currently likely to be [[Sobraca]] or [[Character Creation]].  

There is also an [[Index of Pages]]. Which has a link to every page on this site. Feel free to use it or the search bar to try and find what you are looking for quickly.  

For those new to Pathfinder 2E here is a link to the [Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/PlayersGuide.aspx)! Which is a free source compendium for all Pathfinder books and rules for everyone to utilize.  

For those new to the campaign here is a link to a [Safety & Consent Checklist](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeSkgb7ij7W6ityQbf2txB5oxLa-V-iPMPIrCgcQpv59U0Rnw/viewform?usp=dialog). It is just to make sure everyone can say what they are and aren't comfortable with in the story. This particular safety and consent checklist was taken from a different campaign - Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden, but works well for our game too.    

As always questions, comments, and suggestions are welcome!
